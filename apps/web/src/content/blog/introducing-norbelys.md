Cold email still works. What stopped working is the way most teams send it: a blast from one inbox, the same "quick question" to a thousand people and four follow-ups nobody asked for.

Norbelys is built for the other way. Personal emails from your own inboxes, sent at a human pace, with follow-ups that stop the moment someone answers. Every reply lands in one place, so you can spend your day on conversations instead of spreadsheets.

## Why we built it

Every sales team we talked to had the same stack: one tool to send, another to warm up inboxes, a third to verify the list, an AI add-on for first lines and a shared inbox nobody checked. Each one billed separately, and none of them talked to each other.

We wanted one place that does the whole job, at a price you don't have to run past finance.

## What you get for $20 a month

Everything. There are no tiers that hold features back and no AI bill on top.

- **Campaigns and sequences.** Multi-step sequences with A/B tests on any step, business-hours sending and a daily cap for every inbox.
- **A unified inbox.** Replies from every mailbox you connect, threaded with the email that started them.
- **Warm-up and deliverability guardrails.** New inboxes ramp up slowly, dead addresses get caught before you hit send and unsubscribes stick across every campaign.
- **AI personalization.** A custom first line for every prospect, written only from the facts you pick.
- **Open and click tracking** on your own tracking domain.
- **Webhooks, an API and an MCP server**, so Claude or any MCP-ready assistant can work with your campaigns.

## Built around replies

The goal of a cold email isn't to be sent. It's to be answered. So Norbelys treats a reply as the most important thing that can happen.

The second a real person replies, their sequence stops, and you can pause their coworkers at the same company too. Out-of-office notes and auto-replies are flagged, so the people who actually wrote back rise to the top of your inbox.

## What's next

Three products are on the way: **Mail** for one-off emails from any connected inbox, **Broadcasts** for newsletters to the people who said yes and **Meetings** to turn "let's talk" into a booked call.
